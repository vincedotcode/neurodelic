# Surface brief: neurodelic.vincedotcode.com

Scope: the library site (home, gallery, piece editor, icons, type, UI kit, guide, use). Visitor mode: Persuade on the home page, Operate everywhere else.

Audience: founders, designers and developers shopping for illustration for a product about thinking. Job: find the right picture for a product moment, tune it to their brand, copy it into their code or design file. Proof: the art itself, live and endless, plus the code to use it.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Quiet body, loud mind. The interface is the quiet body (paper, one blue-black ink, ballpoint annotations); the art is the only loud thing on the page. It refuses the category default (a white grid of rounded pastel cards under a purple gradient hero) and the AI default (near-black with a neon glow).

OWN-WORLD: A proof print marked up in ballpoint. Cool paper ground, blue-black ink for text, one ballpoint blue for every mark a hand would make: underlines, circles round the active thing, arrows, focus rings, the text selection. Bricolage Grotesque, condensed and heavy for display, as text for body; a mono only for code. Components have a double hand-drawn edge. Art sits in plates with a thin paper margin, never in rounded cards.

STORY: The visitor watches one moment change through eight states of mind, understands it is a genre with a grammar, browses 1,024 pieces, opens one, tunes and copies it, then finds the icons, the type and the UI kit drawn in the same hand.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: left five columns hold the thesis as the h1, two lines in Vince's voice, and the two actions (browse all 1,024, make your own). Right seven columns hold one large live plate with eight small live swatches under it, one per state; clicking a swatch redraws the same moment in that state with a reveal that spreads from where the thought starts. A ballpoint arrow in Scrawl points at the swatches. Mobile: thesis, plate, swatches, then copy and actions.

FORM: Proof sheet with ballpoint markup (first on the ordered list). seed: none (unavailable).

FINISH: Unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance (all rasters are rendered by the repo's own code).
