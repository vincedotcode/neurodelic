# Neurodelic: product

## What it is

Neurodelic is an illustration genre for the moments in a product when someone is thinking: loading, focus, an empty page, an error, the moment it clicks. It ships as a library people can browse, tune, copy and download: 1,024 curated illustrations and an endless generator, 61 icons in four hands, the Scrawl typeface and a UI kit drawn in the same hand. Everything is drawn by code. No stock, no image models.

## The message

**Quiet body, loud mind.**

Every Neurodelic picture has two parts. The body is quiet: one still figure, a solid silhouette or a few lines. The mind is loud: whatever is happening inside that person, shown as texture and colour around and through them.

## The visual problem it solves

Product illustration shows the outside of people. Flat figures with smiling faces carry boxes, point at charts and high-five next to a laptop. Products about thinking (AI, learning, focus, mental health, creative tools) get a lightbulb, a gear in a head, a glowing orb or a sparkle. None of it shows what thinking feels like.

Neurodelic draws the inside instead. A feeling is never an object. It is a way of making marks:

| State | Texture | Feels like |
|---|---|---|
| Tangle | Contour lines, coloured scribble | A thought you can't stop thinking |
| Thermal | False-colour scan bands | Seeing the heat of a feeling |
| Stipple | Dot shading under a deep sky | Standing still while everything moves |
| Haze | Film grain, soft blur | Letting go and falling into it |
| Collage | Cut paper, halftone, print grain | Daydreaming through space |
| Burst | Paint shards, ink splatter | Too much, all at once |
| Ember | Pen hatching, flame | A feeling that burns |
| Wall | Handwriting, everywhere | Every thought written down at once |

Any product moment maps to one of 32 moments (new idea, overload, searching, broken, waiting, welcome and more), and every moment exists in all eight states.

## Who it is for

Founders, designers and developers building products where thinking is the point: AI tools, learning, focus and wellbeing apps, creative software, research tools. They want illustration that feels like their product and not like everyone else's, and they want it in a format they can ship today: SVG, React, PNG, a script tag, or an agent skill.

## Brand commitments

- The figure stays quiet. No faces with features, no smiles, no props.
- The inside is never an object: no lightbulbs, gears, circuit brains, sparkles or robot heads.
- Every surface is made of visible marks: lines, dots, hatching, grain, shards, letters.
- Everything is original and drawn by code. Reference images were for style direction only.
- Copy reads as Vince writing in the first person.
- Free to use, personal or commercial. Not to be resold as an asset pack.

## Constraints

- Public repo. No secrets, no private links, no internal notes.
- Zero runtime dependencies. Plain ES modules that run in the browser and Node 18+.
- Static hosting on Vercel at neurodelic.vincedotcode.com.
- Every illustration is deterministic: same moment, state, seed and options, same SVG.
